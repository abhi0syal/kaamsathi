# KaamSathi - Pending Verification & State-by-State Rules

This file documents scheme rules and details that were intentionally excluded from `/data/kb.json` pending formal gazette notifications or official clarification from respective government departments.

## 1. Code on Social Security 2020: Aggregator Contribution Percentage
- **Status**: The central rules under the Code on Social Security, 2020 provide for a contribution range between 1% and 2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of payments to gig workers.
- **Pending**: Final gazette notification specifying the exact percentage and date of implementation for all state-level social security boards across India.
- **Safe handling in KaamSathi**: Stated as statutory cap in summary; no speculative enforcement dates or exact rates promised to workers.

## 2. Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Act 2023: Exact Per-Transaction Cess Rate
- **Status**: Section 12 allows the Welfare Board to levy a welfare fee/cess up to 2% per transaction.
- **Pending**: State government issuance of specific category-wise transaction fee schedule (food delivery vs ride-hailing).
- **Safe handling in KaamSathi**: Highlighted that aggregators contribute to the welfare fund, and workers get Unique IDs and welfare coverage without deducting from their net fare.

## 3. Karnataka Gig Worker Insurance Scheme: Expansion to Non-Delivery Gig Roles
- **Status**: Currently explicitly covers platform food delivery and e-commerce delivery partners up to ₹4 lakh composite cover.
- **Pending**: Clear departmental gazette expansion covering on-demand home service workers (e.g., plumbers, beauticians, cleaning services).
- **Safe handling in KaamSathi**: Excluded from claiming full coverage for home services until officially notified.

## 4. PM-JAY Senior Citizen Top-up (70+ Age) Operational Guidelines
- **Status**: Union Cabinet approved coverage for all citizens 70+ irrespective of income.
- **Pending**: Verification of hospital desk procedure differences between SECC cardholders vs non-SECC senior citizens.
- **Safe handling in KaamSathi**: Highlighted age eligibility (70+) and directed users to verify on `beneficiary.nha.gov.in`.
